The Dialine Ritual

Four movements toward calm.

  1. 01

    Arrive

    Slip away from the day with a welcome tea, a warm towel and a quiet moment to set your intention.

  2. 02

    Consult

    Your therapist listens, then tailors pressure, aroma and pace to your body and mood.

  3. 03

    Restore

    Surrender to your chosen treatment in a private suite lit by candle and shadow.

  4. 04

    Linger

    Rest in our thermal lounge with herbal infusions as the calm settles into you.
